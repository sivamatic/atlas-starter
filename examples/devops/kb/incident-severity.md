## Levels
SEV1: customers cannot check out or log in, or data is at risk. SEV2: a major feature is degraded for many customers. SEV3: a minor feature is degraded or a workaround exists.

## Communication
For SEV1 and SEV2, the incident commander posts an update in the incident channel every 30 minutes and updates the public status page. SEV3 needs a ticket, not a status page update.
