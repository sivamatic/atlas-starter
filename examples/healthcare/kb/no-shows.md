## Recording a no-show
Mark the appointment as a no-show 15 minutes after the start time if the patient has not arrived or called.

## Repeated no-shows
After 2 no-shows in 6 months, call the patient to check if they need help getting to the clinic, such as transport or a reminder by text. The clinic does not charge a no-show fee.
