## Verifying identity
Before you discuss anything about a patient, verify two identifiers: full name and date of birth. On the phone, never confirm that someone is a patient until both match.

## Minimum necessary
Look at a patient record only when your work needs it. Never send patient information by personal email or chat. Use the secure messaging system.

## Family and carers
Share information with family or carers only when the patient's consent is documented in the record, or when the clinician decides it is in the patient's interest in an emergency.
