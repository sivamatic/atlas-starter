## Submitting claims
Submit insurance claims within 5 business days of the visit. Check that the visit codes match the clinician's note before you submit.

## Denied claims
For a denied claim, read the denial reason code, fix the claim and resubmit within 30 days. If the denial says the service is not covered, send the patient a cost estimate letter before you bill them.
