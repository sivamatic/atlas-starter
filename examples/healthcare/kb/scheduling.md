## New patient appointments
Book new patients into a 40-minute slot. Existing patients get a 20-minute slot unless the clinician asks for longer. Book in the scheduling system, never on paper.

## Same-day appointments
Same-day slots are released at 8:00 each morning. Offer them first to patients with urgent symptoms, then to anyone on the waiting list.

## Interpreters
If a patient needs an interpreter, book one when you book the appointment and add 20 minutes to the slot.
