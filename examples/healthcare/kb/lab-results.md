## Normal results
A clinician reviews every result. Normal results are released to the patient portal within 3 business days of review.

## Abnormal results
The clinician calls the patient about an abnormal result before it appears in the portal. If the clinician cannot reach the patient in 2 days, a nurse sends a secure message asking them to call.
