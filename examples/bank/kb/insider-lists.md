## Adding a person to an insider list
Compliance adds a person to the insider list within one hour of their access to inside information. Record the time, the reason and the information they received.

## Restricted securities
Securities on the restricted list cannot be recommended or traded for clients. Advisors see a block in the order system; only compliance sees the reason.
