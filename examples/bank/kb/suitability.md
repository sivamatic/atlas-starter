## When it is required
Complete a suitability assessment before you recommend any investment product, including funds and structured notes. Deposits like the Fixed Saver do not need one.

## Risk profile questionnaire
The risk profile questionnaire is valid for 24 months, or until the client reports a major life change such as retirement, a new dependant or a large inheritance. Record the answers in the advice system before the meeting ends.

## Vulnerable clients
If a client shows signs of vulnerability, such as confusion, bereavement or pressure from a third party, pause the advice, offer a second meeting and log a vulnerability flag. Never complete a sale in the same meeting.
