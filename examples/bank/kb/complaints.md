## Logging a complaint
Log every complaint in the complaints register the same day, even if you solved it on the spot. A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, spoken or written.

## Deadlines
Send a written acknowledgement within 2 business days. Send the final response within 8 weeks. If you cannot meet the deadline, write to the client before it passes and explain why.
