## Annual management fee
The standard annual management fee is 0.85 percent on the first 2 million EUR and 0.60 percent above that. The fee is charged quarterly in arrears.

## Fee discounts
A discount of up to 0.15 percent needs approval from the desk head. Larger discounts need the regional head of private banking.
