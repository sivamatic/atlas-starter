## Product summary
The Fixed Saver is a 12-month fixed-rate deposit. The minimum deposit is 1,000 EUR and the maximum is 500,000 EUR per client. The rate is fixed on the day the money arrives and is published every Monday on the rates page.

## Early withdrawal
Clients can close a Fixed Saver early only once, for the full balance. The early withdrawal penalty is 90 days of interest. Partial withdrawals are not allowed.

## Who can open one
Any client aged 18 or over with a Corvane current account. Business clients use the Business Deposit product instead.
