Meridian staff connect to internal systems through GlobalConnect VPN.

## Symptoms
The VPN disconnects every few minutes, or pages load slowly while connected. Since 27 October this is usually incident INC-2041, which affects the US-West gateway.

## Workaround during INC-2041
Open GlobalConnect, choose Settings, then Gateway, and select US-East. Disconnect and connect again. US-East is not affected by INC-2041. Switch back to Automatic when the incident is closed on the status page.

## If it still drops
Restart your laptop, then check that you are on the latest GlobalConnect version (6.2 or later). If the VPN still drops on US-East, ask Atlas to open a ticket and include the time it dropped.
