## New phone
Before you wipe your old phone, open the Authenticator app and move your account to the new phone. If the old phone is already gone, you need an MFA reset.

## Getting an MFA reset
An MFA reset needs a ticket because it removes a security control. Ask Atlas to open a ticket. The service desk calls you back on the number in the staff directory to confirm it is you before resetting. They never ask for your password.
