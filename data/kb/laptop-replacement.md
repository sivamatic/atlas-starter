## Broken or damaged laptop
Open a ticket with a photo of the damage. A loaner laptop is available the same day from the IT desk on floor 3 in Austin and floor 2 in Dublin.

## Replacement cycle
Laptops are replaced every four years. Earlier replacement needs your manager's approval in the ticket.
