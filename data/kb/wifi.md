## Which network to use
Use the Meridian-Corp network with your Meridian sign-in. Meridian-Guest is for visitors only and cannot reach internal systems.

## Cannot connect to Meridian-Corp
Forget the network, then connect again and sign in. If your password changed in the last day, sign out of your laptop and back in first so the new password is used.
