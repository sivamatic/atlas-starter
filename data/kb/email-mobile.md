## Set up
Install Outlook from your phone's app store and sign in with your Meridian account. Your phone must have a screen lock. Other mail apps are blocked by policy.

## Email stopped syncing
Open Outlook, go to Settings, choose your account and select Reset account. This keeps your mail on the server.
